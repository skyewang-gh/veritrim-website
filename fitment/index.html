How to read the answer

Two words do all the work on this site: confirmed and pending. Here is what each one means.

Candidate filter

We have the part, we are still checking the fit

The filter, the model and the year range come from our catalogue, built against the OEM housing. What is not done yet is the check on a physical vehicle. Until that is done we label every fit as pending - we would rather say so than sell you a guess.

No confirmed fit

We would rather tell you nothing than something wrong

If a filter does not fit your vehicle, or we do not have one that does, this checker says so and tells you why. That includes the years where the housing changed under the same model name.

What we do not have an answer for yet

Honesty beats coverage. Here is the list of what is still open, so you know before you order.

  • •Hybrid variants of the Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Sienna and Yaris - the hybrid housing can differ from the standard one. Send us your VIN and we will check the part number for you.
  • •The Prius, Prius v, Prius Prime and Lexus CT 200h families. We do not have a verified fit for them right now.
  • •The Lexus UX 250h, which does not take the Toyota filter in this range.
  • •Any BMW other than the X3 G01, and any Mercedes-Benz other than the GLC X253. The one exception worth knowing: the electric BMW iX3 (G08, model years 2021-2025 only) does take the same filter, but it was never sold new in Canada - so a Canadian iX3 is a parallel import - and it went out of production in March 2025. The 2026-onward iX3 is a different car (NA5) and this filter does not fit it. It is not in this checker because it is not a Canadian model; send us the VIN and we will confirm.
  • •Trim-level differences within a model year. If your trim is not listed, send us your VIN before ordering.

Need a human?

One business day, usually less.

  • Send us your year, make, model and trim - or the VIN, which is better.
  • Send us the part number printed on your old filter. That settles it faster than anything else.
  • We will tell you if the fit is confirmed, if it is not, and if it is somewhere we cannot answer yet.
