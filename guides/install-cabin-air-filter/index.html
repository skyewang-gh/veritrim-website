Install guide

Changing a cabin air filter

Ten minutes, no tools, one filter. This page covers the steps that are the same on every car we sell, and the places where a specific model does something different.

The steps

  1. 1

    Open the glove box

    Reach up under the dashboard and release the clips holding the glove box, then let it drop down.

  2. 2

    Take off the filter cover

    The cabin filter sits in a housing behind the glove box. Slide the cover off its rails.

  3. 3

    Pull out the old filter

    Before you take it out, look at the arrow printed on the frame. That is the direction air has to travel.

  4. 4

    Slide the new filter in

    Same rails, same direction. It should seat flat. If it will not seat, the orientation is wrong - do not force it.

  5. 5

    Put everything back

    Refit the cover, push the glove box back up and click the clips closed.

Before you start

  • Write down or photograph the part number on your old filter before you remove it.
  • Photograph which way the arrow points while the old filter is still in place.
  • Keep the old filter - you may want to compare it, and it tells us the part number if something does not seat.

Do not do these

  • Do not wash the filter. Water destroys the activated carbon layer, and it does not come back.
  • Do not run the blower or the air conditioning with the housing cover off. Unfiltered air goes straight into the cabin.
  • Do not force a filter that will not slide in. A filter that is a fraction too big, or the wrong way round, is how a housing gets damaged.
  • Do not reuse a filter that has been in the car. Once it is out, it is a spent part.

Model-specific notes

Where a model does something different from the standard steps, we spell it out here.

BMW X3 G01, 2018-2024

Behind the glove box. Release the two clips, lower the glove box, slide the cover off, swap the filter. The arrow points down.

Toyota, 2006-2019 platforms

Behind the passenger-side glove box. Lower the glove box, remove the filter cover, slide out the old filter, swap. The arrow points down for these models - confirm it against your old filter.

Mercedes-Benz GLC X253, 2016-2023

Passenger side, below the glove box. The engine-bay filter on the same platform is a different part with a different size - this guide is not about that one.

When to replace it

A cabin filter is a cheap part that does an expensive job.

Every 12 months

Replace at least once a year, or at every oil service - whichever comes first.

Sooner in bad conditions

Dusty roads, heavy pollen, coastal salt air or stop-and-go traffic: change it before the year is up.

When the airflow drops

Weak airflow at the vents, a musty smell when the air conditioning starts, or a windscreen that will not clear quickly - all signs it is time.
