We are here to help with your warranty, returns, and product questions. Most inquiries are answered within 1-2 business days.

Warranty & Returns

Warranty Policy

  • All Veritrim products carry a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase against defects in materials and workmanship.
  • Warranty does not cover damage resulting from improper installation, misuse, abuse, accident, or normal wear and tear.
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) is required for all warranty claims.
  • Warranty remedy is limited to replacement of the defective product at our discretion. Shipping costs for warranty replacements may be the responsibility of the buyer.
  • To initiate a warranty claim, use the contact form on this page and select "Warranty Claim" as the request type.

Returns Policy

  • Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Product must be unused, unopened, and in its original packaging to be eligible for return.
  • Return shipping is the responsibility of the buyer unless the item is defective or was shipped in error.
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.
  • Custom or special-order items cannot be returned.
  • To start a return, use the contact form on this page and select "Return Request" as the request type. Include your order number for faster processing.
